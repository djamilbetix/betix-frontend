Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information Collection

Betix collects the following information: your Pi Network username, Pi wallet address, events you create, tickets you purchase. We do not collect sensitive information.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used to: create and manage events, process your ticket purchases, send you confirmations, and improve our services.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device and on our secure servers. Transactions are recorded on the Pi Network blockchain.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is shared only with Pi Network for payments and with event organizers for ticket validation.

5. Your Rights

You can access, modify, or delete your data via your Betix profile. You can also contact us with any questions.

6. Security

We use the Pi Network blockchain to secure your transactions. Your Pi wallet is protected by your own private key.
