Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

Betix collects your Pi Network username, your Pi wallet address, the events you create, and the tickets you purchase. We do not collect your confidential information. We never ask for your Pi password, passphrases, email address, or any other sensitive personal information. Login to our platform is done exclusively through Pi Network without us having access to your credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to create and manage events, process your ticket purchases, send you transaction confirmations, and improve our platform services. We may also use your data to communicate important updates about your account or events you are participating in.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your device and on our secure servers. All transactions are permanently recorded on the Pi Network blockchain, which provides transparency and security. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your information.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is shared only with Pi Network for payment processing and with event organizers for ticket validation and attendance tracking. We require all third parties to maintain the confidentiality of your information.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time through your Betix profile settings. You may also request a copy of your data or ask us to restrict processing. For any data-related requests, please contact us directly.

6. Security Measures

We use the Pi Network blockchain technology to secure your transactions. Your Pi wallet is protected by your own private key, which we never access or store. We have no knowledge of your passwords or passphrases. We regularly review our security practices to ensure your data remains protected against unauthorized access.

7. Contact Us

For any questions regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact us at betixservices@gmail.com. You may also reach us through our official social media channels at @betix_app.
