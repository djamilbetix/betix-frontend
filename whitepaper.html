2. PI NETWORK: THE ECOSYSTEM POWERING BETIX

2.1 What is Pi Network?

Pi Network is a mobile cryptocurrency platform created in 2019 by a team of Stanford University doctors. Its goal is to make blockchain technology accessible to everyone by allowing users to mine Pi directly from their smartphones, without excessive energy consumption. With over 60 million active users worldwide, Pi Network is one of the most important and promising blockchain projects of the decade.

Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies that require expensive hardware and massive electricity consumption, Pi Network uses a consensus algorithm based on trust and social circles, making it eco-friendly and accessible. The Pi currency is currently in enclosed mainnet phase, with an opening to open mainnet planned in the coming months, enabling external transactions and increased valuation.

Betix is proudly built on Pi Network because we share common values: financial inclusion, decentralization, accessibility and positive social impact. By using Pi as a payment currency, Betix allows millions of unbanked people to participate in the global event economy.

2.2 The Pi Network Founders

  • Dr. Nicolas Kokkalis – Co-founder, Chief Technology Officer
  • Dr. Chengdiao Fan – Co-founder, Chief Strategy Officer

Nicolas Kokkalis is an expert in distributed systems and blockchain technologies. A Stanford University Ph.D., he led the technical development of Pi Network, including the Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP) adapted for smartphones.

Chengdiao Fan is an expert in digital anthropology and user experience design. She designed Pi Network's growth strategy and community approach, which achieved over 60 million users in just a few years.

2.3 Pi Network's Blockchain Technology

Pi Network is based on the Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP), an efficient and secure consensus algorithm that does not require intensive mining. Unlike Proof of Work (PoW) used by Bitcoin, SCP enables fast, low-cost transactions with a minimal carbon footprint. This technology is ideal for mobile use and for low-value transactions like ticket purchases.

The Pi network is currently in Enclosed Mainnet phase, meaning transactions are secured and recorded on the blockchain, but external exchanges are limited to ensure stability and security. This phase allows testing and refining features before full mainnet opening, expected in 2026 or 2027.

Pi Network's security is reinforced by the Security Circles system, which allows users to guarantee network trust by validating transactions from their trusted contacts. This innovative mechanism combines decentralization with a human approach to trust.

2.4 Identity Verification (KYC) on Pi Network

Pi Network has an integrated identity verification (KYC) system, which helps fight fraud and ensure account uniqueness. This process is essential for platform security and regulatory compliance. Users who wish to make significant transactions or use Pi services must undergo this verification.

Pi's KYC uses facial recognition technology and identity document verification, with decentralized processing that protects user privacy. This system is also used by Betix to ensure that every organizer and buyer is a real person, significantly reducing fraud and fake ticket risks.

2.5 The Pi Network Ecosystem and Betix

Pi Network is not just a cryptocurrency; it is a true ecosystem of decentralized services. Developers can create applications (apps) on Pi using the Pi API, and users can use their Pi to pay for goods and services. Betix is one of the first ticketing applications built on this ecosystem.

The Pi ecosystem also includes the Pi Browser, which allows access to decentralized applications (dApps), and the Pi Wallet, which secures users' funds. Betix is integrated with these tools, offering a smooth and secure ticket purchasing experience.

By leveraging the Pi ecosystem, Betix benefits from a massive user base, robust infrastructure and an engaged community. We also contribute to enriching this ecosystem by bringing a new utility to the Pi currency: purchasing tickets for real-world events.

3. PROBLEMATIC AND SOLUTION

3.1 Current market difficulties

Today, organizing or participating in an event presents several major problems that hinder the development of the sector and exclude many people from ticket purchasing. These difficulties are structural and affect the entire event ticketing industry.

Excessive transaction fees

Traditional platforms charge up to 20% commission on each ticket sold. On €100, the organizer sometimes receives only €80. These commissions, often justified by management fees, represent a major barrier for small organizers and associative events operating with tight budgets.

Very long payment delays

Funds take between 3 and 7 days to become available in the organizer's account. This latency complicates payment to artists, service providers and suppliers, creating unnecessary financial tensions and payment delays that can compromise the event's success.

Massive financial exclusion

Nearly 2 billion people worldwide do not have a bank account and therefore cannot buy tickets online. This exclusion particularly affects populations in Africa, Southeast Asia and Latin America, depriving them of access to culture and entertainment.

Lack of transparency and fraud

Fake ticket sales are common. The fraud rate sometimes reaches 12% for major events. Participants are scammed and organizers lose credibility. The lack of transaction traceability worsens this phenomenon and undermines consumer confidence.

Illegal resale and speculation

Tickets are resold on parallel platforms at prices three times higher than their original price. This phenomenon excludes genuine fans and creates a black market harmful to all. Ticket speculation has become a scourge that distorts the spirit of events.

3.2 The Betix solution

Betix addresses these problems with a decentralized and transparent approach, leveraging the Pi Network blockchain. Our platform eliminates intermediaries, reduces costs, secures transactions and guarantees ticket authenticity through unique and unforgeable QR codes.

Betix allows organizers to receive their funds instantly in Pi, without processing delays. Participants pay only the ticket price, with no hidden fees. The blockchain guarantees full traceability of each transaction, making fraud impossible.

Our solution is accessible to all, with a simple Pi Network wallet. No bank account is required, opening access to events for millions of unbanked people. Betix is thus a vector of cultural and social inclusion.

Finally, thanks to Pi Network's KYC verification system, we ensure that every organizer and participant is a real person, eliminating the risks of fraudulent accounts and reinforcing trust within the community.
