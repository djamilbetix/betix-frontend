Introduction

Betix is the first decentralized event ticketing platform built on the Pi Network blockchain. Our mission is to democratize access to cultural and sporting events by providing a secure, transparent, and accessible platform for everyone.

By leveraging the power of the Pi Network, Betix eliminates intermediaries, reduces costs, and guarantees the authenticity of tickets through blockchain technology.

Objectives

  • Accessibility: Make event ticketing accessible to everyone, everywhere, using Pi cryptocurrency.
  • Security: Guarantee secure and transparent transactions through blockchain technology.
  • Decentralization: Eliminate intermediaries to offer fair prices to buyers and organizers.
  • Community: Build a vibrant community of event enthusiasts and organizers.
  • Innovation: Continuously improve the platform with new features and technologies.

Pi Network Integration

Betix is built on the Pi Network ecosystem, using Pi cryptocurrency for all transactions. This integration allows:

  • Fast transactions: Payments are processed in seconds with minimal fees.
  • Global accessibility: Anyone with a Pi wallet can buy or sell tickets worldwide.
  • Security: Transactions are recorded on the blockchain, ensuring transparency and traceability.
  • Escrow system: Funds are secured until event validation, protecting both buyers and sellers.
