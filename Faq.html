Frequently Asked Questions

Q What is Betix?
Betix is the first decentralized event ticketing platform built on the Pi Network blockchain.
Q How does Betix work?
Betix uses the Pi Network blockchain to ensure secure and transparent transactions.
Q Is Betix free?
Yes! Betix is completely free for users. No commission on sales.
Q Who can use Betix?
Any Pi Network account holder can use Betix.
Q How to buy a ticket?
Connect, browse events, click 'Buy Ticket' and choose the quantity.
Q Are payments secure?
Yes, via the Pi Network and Betix's escrow system.
Q Can I get a refund?
Yes in case of cancellation, postponement or fraud.
Q Where are my tickets stored?
In the 'My Tickets' section of your account.
Q How to create an event?
Connect, click 'Create Event' and fill out the form.
Q Conditions to be an organizer?
Have an active Pi Network account and comply with the terms of use.
Q Can I modify an event?
Yes, from the 'My Events' section.
Q How to boost my event?
By paying a small amount in Pi to increase visibility.
Q How to connect my Pi account?
Click 'Connect Pi' in the menu and authorize access.
Q What is the escrow system?
A mechanism that blocks funds until event validation.
Q Are transactions anonymous?
Transactions are traceable on the blockchain, but your information remains private.
Q Other cryptocurrencies?
Currently only Pi Network.
Q Buyer protection?
Via escrow, organizer verification and a refund policy.
Q Report a problem?
Via chat, email or social networks.
Q Contact support?
Online chat, email or Telegram.
Q Available languages?
English, French, Portuguese, Chinese, Indonesian.
Q Join the community?
Follow us on Telegram, Twitter, Discord, Instagram and WhatsApp.
Q Become an ambassador?
Contact us to apply for the ambassador program.
Q Become a partner?
Contact us to discuss partnership opportunities.
Q Future projects?
Mobile app, new cryptocurrencies, social features.
